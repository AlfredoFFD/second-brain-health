second-brain-health

second-brain-health is a single-user, personal tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and reads the owner's own sleep, activity and health-metric data from the Google Health API. It then saves a daily summary into the owner's private notes, so the owner can track their own sleep and training.

It is not offered to the public. No one other than the owner uses it, and it has no accounts, ads or servers.
